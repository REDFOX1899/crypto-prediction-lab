How bids become asks

A YES bid is an offer to buy YES. A NO bid is an offer to buy NO. Kalshi publishes bids for both sides.

A 40¢ NO bid corresponds to a 60¢ YES ask, with the same quantity: 100¢ − 40¢ = 60¢. Similarly, a 35¢ YES bid corresponds to a 65¢ NO ask.

These are displayed prices before fees; the available quantity can change.
